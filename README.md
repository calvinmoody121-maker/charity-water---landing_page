# charity: water Landing Page — Project Feedback

Summary of instructor feedback on the first landing page project.

## What went well

- **Brand palette and typography:** The design used charity: water’s approved colors and type. The blue palette connected to water and the feeling the page was meant to create.
- **Landing page visuals:** The page felt like a real landing page, with navigation, the charity: water logo, multiple images, and a clear visual hierarchy.
- **LevelUp (LinkedIn):** The extra step of sharing the work on LinkedIn and reflecting on designing for an audience was completed.

## Where to improve

- **Audience targeting:** Narrow the audience to college students and cover all five criteria: who they are, what they’re looking for, what influences them, their worries, and what moves them to act.
- **Value proposition and headline:** Keep the “saving lives” message, but tie it more clearly to what makes charity: water unique — transparency, trackable impact, and the 100% model.
- **Photo choice:** The image is selected, but the write-up should explain how it connects to the brand’s focus on hope, dignity, and possibility.
- **Messaging:** Speak more directly to college students and what motivates them to give, so the copy matches the audience strategy.
- **CTA:** “Save” is unclear as the main action. Use something like “Donate Now” or “Give Today.”
